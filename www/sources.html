Nutrition & Dietary Guidelines

Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2025-2030

U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) & U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)

Federal nutrition policy guidance on recommended daily servings, calorie-based dietary patterns, and limits on processed foods and added sugars.

www.dietaryguidelines.gov

Healthy Diet Fact Sheet

World Health Organization (WHO)

International dietary guidance on intake of fruits, vegetables, fats, sugars, and salt for a global audience.

who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet

Dietary Reference Intakes (DRIs)

National Institutes of Health (NIH), Office of Dietary Supplements

Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDAs), Adequate Intakes, and Tolerable Upper Intake Levels for vitamins and minerals.

ods.od.nih.gov

Health & Wellness

Diet and Lifestyle Recommendations

American Heart Association (AHA)

Heart-healthy eating patterns, sodium intake limits, and recommendations on saturated fat, cholesterol, and added sugars.

heart.org

Health Information Library

Mayo Clinic

Evidence-based information on diseases, conditions, nutrition, and wellness topics reviewed by physician medical editors.

mayoclinic.org

BMI & Calorie Calculations

BMI Calculator and Classification

National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI), NIH

Standard BMI formula and classification categories: underweight (<18.5), normal (18.5-24.9), overweight (25-29.9), obese (≥30).

nhlbi.nih.gov/calculate-your-bmi

Adult BMI Calculator

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

BMI calculation methodology and interpretation for adults.

cdc.gov/bmi

Mifflin-St Jeor Equation for Resting Energy Expenditure

Mifflin MD, St Jeor ST, et al. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 1990;51(2):241-247

Validated predictive equation for estimating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) used in daily calorie need calculations.

pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711

Physical Activity

Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 2nd Edition

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)

Recommended 150 min/week moderate-intensity aerobic activity plus 2+ days muscle-strengthening for adults.

cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics

Sleep & Mental Wellness

About Sleep

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

Recommended sleep durations for adults (at least 7 hours per night) and health risks of insufficient sleep.

cdc.gov/sleep

Recommended Amount of Sleep for a Healthy Adult

American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) & Sleep Research Society (SRS)

Clinical consensus statement that adults should sleep 7 or more hours per night on a regular basis.

pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4434546

Sleep Duration Recommendations

National Sleep Foundation (NSF)

Age-specific sleep duration ranges including adults (7-9 hours), older adults (7-8 hours), and teens (8-10 hours).

thensf.org
