WHAT SADIKY DOES:
Sadiky is a voice-powered AI health companion that helps users track medications,
analyze meals via photos, monitor daily health metrics from Apple Health, and maintain
wellness routines through conversational AI. Designed for all ages including elderly
users — large buttons, voice-first interaction, no typing required.

NATIVE FEATURES (beyond WebView):
1. Native Speech Recognition — Uses iOS SFSpeechRecognizer for voice input
   (not available in WKWebView). Tap the microphone button in the chat to test.
   Supports multiple languages via device locale.
2. Apple HealthKit Integration — Reads steps, calories, heart rate, sleep, and weight
   from Apple Health (read-only, no write access). Visible on the dashboard.
3. StoreKit 2 In-App Purchase — Native subscription handling with Apple IAP.
4. Sign in with Apple — Redirect-based web authentication with JWT signature
   verification against Apple's JWKS.
5. Haptic Feedback — Native haptics on UI interactions.

HOW TO TEST VOICE:
1. Open the app and sign in (or use Demo mode)
2. Navigate to Chat (tap "Talk to Sadiky" from the + menu)
3. Tap the microphone button
4. Grant microphone and speech recognition permissions when prompted
5. Speak naturally — transcription appears in real-time
6. The AI responds to your spoken message

HOW TO TEST MEAL PHOTO:
1. Tap the + button (center of bottom nav) and select "Snap a Meal"
2. Take a photo of any meal or select from photo library
3. The AI analyzes the image and returns calorie/macro estimates

HOW TO TEST ACCOUNT DELETION:
1. Sign in with any method
2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
3. Scroll to Account section
4. Tap "Delete Account"
5. Confirm deletion — all server-side data is permanently removed
